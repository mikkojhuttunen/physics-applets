Laser cavity workbench

Place mirrors, crystals, etalons and filters in order around the beam path. Beam size comes from ABCD matrices (tangential and sagittal); threshold, mode selection and linewidth follow from the round-trip loss and the spectral filters.

Model assumptions – read before trusting a number
  • Optics. Paraxial ABCD matrices in reduced distances (d/n), so the q-parameter eigenvalue of the round trip directly gives the physical beam radius. Curved mirrors at incidence angle θ: fT = (R/2)cos θ, fS = R/(2cos θ). Brewster-cut slabs: effective distances L/n³ (tangential) and L/n (sagittal), with L the path length inside the crystal (Kogelnik).
  • Thermal lens. Thin lens at the crystal centre, f = πKwp²/(Pheat·dn/dT) with Pheat = quantum defect × absorbed pump. It ignores end-face bulging, stress birefringence and aberrations, and overestimates the lens for very tight pump spots, so use the calibration factor if you have a measured value. The Ti:sapphire example uses 0.3 as an assumption, not a measurement.
  • Threshold and power. Ideal four-level rate-equation estimate: Pth = hνpπ(wl²+wp²)γ / (2·Npass·σ·τ), slope = (λp/λl)·ηoverlap·0.9·T/γ. No excited-state absorption, upconversion or reabsorption, so thresholds are optimistic.
  • Mode selection. Every cavity mode inside the gain window is given a ratio x = (gain)/(round-trip loss incl. filter losses). The best mode wins. In a travelling-wave ring (ring + optical diode) homogeneous broadening allows only that one mode; the margin to the runner-up tells you how prone it is to mode hops. With spatial hole burning (linear cavity, or ring without a diode) modes whose x is within half of the excess over threshold also lase; a twisted-mode pair reduces this to 10 %.
  • Filters. Etalons: Airy function with finesse π√R/(1−R), each face of reflectivity R, tilt and tune shift the comb. Lyot filter: plates of thickness d, 2d, 4d in quartz (Δn = 0.0092), T = Π cos²(πτj(ν−νpeak)). Bandpass: Gaussian. Filters are assumed to sit where the beam is collimated enough; a tilted solid etalon in a strongly diverging beam loses finesse, which is not modelled.
  • Linewidth. Single mode: technical linewidth ν·δp/p (path-length noise) added in quadrature to the Schawlow–Townes limit 2πhν(Δνc)²/P. Multimode: the frequency span of the lasing modes.
