RH = (p μh² − n μe²) / [e (p μh + n μe)²]. In the saturation range RH = ±1/(e|ND − NA|), so the sign gives the doping type and the magnitude the net doping. A p-type sample flips sign at high T because μe > μh.

Model: parabolic bands with density-of-states masses and a Varshni gap Eg(T); Fermi–Dirac statistics for the bands (Bednarczyk–Bednarczyk approximation of F1/2); one level per dopant with spin degeneracy 2 (donor) and 4 (acceptor); neutrality p + ND+ = n + NA− solved by bisection. Mobility: lattice scattering ∝ T−3/2 plus ionized-impurity scattering ∝ T3/2 (Matthiessen), qualitative only; Hall factor set to 1. Left out: impurity band and Mott transition, band-gap narrowing, excited dopant states, magnetic freeze-out.
