From bond to breaking point

One solid, four views. The curvature of the interatomic potential sets the stiffness, defects decide where the solid gives way, and the elastic constants tie the deformation together. The panels share their parameters: change the bond in the first panel and the stress–strain curve, the shear stresses and the block all follow.
