Si, Ge and GaAs use the empirical pseudopotential form factors of Cohen and Bergstresser (1966) with their real lattice constants, so energies are in eV. Diamond uses Si's form factors scaled up (by 1.21) to land near its known gap, and Al uses two approximate form factors. The other lattices are toy models in units of ħ²/2m·(2π/a)². The plane-wave basis is truncated and spin-orbit coupling is ignored, so this teaches the symmetry argument; it is not a substitute for a full band-structure calculation.
