How to play
  • One player draws a card and explains the term; teammates guess. The clock starts when the card is revealed.
  • Timer off? Press Stop timer to play without a clock. Press it again to bring the clock back.
  • New card swaps the card at once and the timer keeps running, but it breaks your streak.
  • Guess three cards in a row and the unicorns come galloping.
  • Do not say the term or any word marked Don't say. No gestures, no spelling, no "rhymes with".
  • Press Guessed it when the team gets it. The card ends the moment the timer hits zero.
  • Use the topic chips to play only the chapters you want to revise. Cards do not repeat until the filtered deck is used up.
