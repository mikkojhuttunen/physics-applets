Curvature steepens the parabolas at left and, since g ∝ 1/√curvature, lowers every DOS and absorption peak. γ → minimum sharpens the peaks toward the ideal 1/√(E−E₀) divergence (tops are clipped at the frame). ΔE moves the subbands apart, and the DOS and absorption peaks follow at the same heights.

Model: Eₙ = ±(E_g/2 + n²ΔE) ± curv·k², g ∝ 1/√(curv·(E−Eₙ+γ)), direct Δn=0 transitions. Booklet §8.2.1, Figs. 8.3–8.4.
