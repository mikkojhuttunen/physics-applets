Reflection, refraction & frustrated total internal reflection

Drag the incidence angle past the critical angle at a glass → air interface and the ray reflects entirely — but bring a second glass block close enough and light tunnels across the gap as an evanescent wave. That's frustrated total internal reflection (FTIR).
