Model notes

Each spectral component is a scalar field A·cos(2πf·t + φ). Amplitude A = √(spectral envelope). Continuous case: f sampled quasi-continuously over the chosen FWHM bandwidth. Discrete case: fn = f₀ + n·Δf with comb spacing Δf = c/2L (cavity free spectral range), so the round-trip period is T = 1/Δf = 2L/c.

A real 1 m Ti:Sapphire cavity comb spans the full ~800–900 nm gain bandwidth with roughly a quarter-million modes 150 MHz apart. This applet can simulate up to ~200,000 comb lines at the exact physical spacing (computed via FFT, so it stays fast even at high N) — enough headroom to dial in realistic 20–50 nm bandwidths and see genuinely short, few-cycle pulses. If N isn't large enough to reach the requested bandwidth, a note appears below the slider.

Transform-limited pulse duration is estimated from τ·Δν ≈ 0.44 (Gaussian), 0.32 (sech²), or 0.89 (flat-top).
