🔭 Image Formation & Ray Bundle Propagation

Thin-lens ray tracing for single- and two-lens imaging systems, plus afocal ray-bundle propagation through Keplerian and Galilean telescope layouts. The plot rescales automatically as image distances grow.

Both are drawn afocal (d = f₁ + f₂): a parallel bundle enters at angle θ, forms a real (Keplerian) or virtual (Galilean) intermediate image at the objective's focal plane, and exits as a parallel bundle at a new angle θ′. Drag the separation slider away from f₁+f₂ to see the output beam stop being parallel — that's exactly what "afocal" means.
