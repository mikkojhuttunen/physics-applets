🌀 Lens Coma Aberration Explorer

Point sources placed off the optical axis get imaged as asymmetric comet-shaped blurs, not sharp points — that's coma. The object arrow on the left is sampled at several heights; each colored dot is a distinct point source traced through the real lens (Snell's law, full pupil sampling), and the matching dot/cloud on the right is its image.
