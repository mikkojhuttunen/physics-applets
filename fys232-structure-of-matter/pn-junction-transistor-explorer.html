Model and notes

Each block is a uniformly doped, fully ionised semiconductor in the abrupt-junction, depletion approximation: the depletion layer of a junction has width w = √(2ε(Vbi − V)(1/NA + 1/ND)/e), and it extends xp = w·ND/(NA+ND) into the p side and xn = w·NA/(NA+ND) into the n side, so the more lightly doped side is depleted deeper. The built-in voltage is eVbi = (Ec − EF)p − (Ec − EF)n = kBT ln(nn0pp0/ni²). The doping and EF − Ei sliders are two views of the same quantity: n0 = nie(EF−Ei)/kBT.

Outside the depletion layers the bands are flat and the minority carriers obey the diffusion equation with the boundary condition of the law of the junction, Δc = c0(eeVj/kBT − 1), at the depletion edge and Δc = 0 at the ohmic end contact. For a block of neutral width W′ this gives the flux (D/L)Δc·coth(W′/L): the long-diode result IS = A·e·ni²(Dp/LpND + Dn/LnNA) for W′ ≫ L and a current ∝ 1/W′ for W′ ≪ L (short diode). The transistor is the same model with two junctions; the base minority-carrier profile has a boundary value at both ends, which reproduces the Ebers–Moll behaviour (active, saturation, cut-off) with no extra assumptions.

Quantities in the transistor readouts: β = IC/IB; emitter injection efficiency γ = electron current injected into the base / total emitter current; base transport factor αT = fraction of the injected carriers that survive the base; α = γαT = IC/IE. To maximise β: dope the emitter much more heavily than the base (γ → 1) and make the base much thinner than its diffusion length (αT → 1). The Early voltage VA = IC/(∂IC/∂VCE) comes from the shrinking neutral base width as the collector depletion layer widens.

Not included, so absolute numbers are idealised: band-gap narrowing and Auger recombination in heavily doped regions (they limit real β to about 50–300 instead of the thousands an ideal 1019 cm−3 emitter would give), incomplete ionisation, degenerate statistics (flagged when doping exceeds 0.1·Nc or 0.1·Nv), high-level injection (flagged), series resistance, and impact ionisation in the transistor. Junction voltages are limited to Vbi − 50 mV where the depletion approximation fails. Reverse breakdown of the diode uses the empirical abrupt-junction estimate VBR ≈ 60 (Eg/1.1 eV)3/2 (N/1016 cm−3)−3/4 V for the lighter side, not less than 4Eg/e, with an avalanche multiplication factor 1/(1 − (V/VBR)³). Mobilities: Caughey–Thomas fit for Si, rougher fits for Ge and GaAs, ∝ T−3/2; the lifetime is the same for all regions. ni follows from Eg(T) (Varshni) and Nc,v ∝ T3/2.
