Notation and derivation

After one round trip starting at P in the cavity (Eq. 1.21–1.23) the flux is F = F₀ (1 − Li)² R₁ R₂ G², with single-pass gain G = eσLΔN. Laser action needs F ≥ F₀, so the condition is (1 − Li)² R₁R₂ e2σLΔN ≥ 1.

Taking the logarithm, with γ₁,₂ = −ln R₁,₂ and γi = −ln(1 − Li), gives 2σLΔN ≥ γ₁ + γ₂ + 2γi ≡ γ, i.e. ΔN ≥ ΔNc = γ/(2σL). Independent loss factors multiply, so their logarithmic losses add.

For R = 1 − T with T ≪ 1 and Li ≪ 1, ln(1 − x) ≈ −x, so γ ≈ T₁ + T₂ + 2Li (Eq. 1.28).

This model has no gain saturation: above threshold the flux grows without bound. Saturation is what fixes the steady-state output power and is treated in the rate-equation applet.
