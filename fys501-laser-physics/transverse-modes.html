Model and conventions

Lengths are in units of the waist radius w₀ and z in units of the Rayleigh range zR = πw₀²/λ. With w(z) = w₀√(1 + z²/zR²), R(z) = z(1 + zR²/z²) and Gouy phase ψ(z) = arctan(z/zR), the fields (time dependence ei(ωt − kz), power-normalised) are

HGmn: E ∝ (w₀/w) Hm(√2x/w) Hn(√2y/w) exp(−r²/w²) · exp(−ikr²/2R) · exp[i(m + n + 1)ψ]

LGpl: E ∝ (w₀/w) (√2r/w)|l| Lp|l|(2r²/w²) exp(−r²/w²) · Φ(φ) · exp(−ikr²/2R) · exp[i(2p + |l| + 1)ψ], with Φ = eilφ (helical), cos lφ or sin lφ (standing).

Hm are the physicists' Hermite polynomials and Lp|l| the generalised Laguerre polynomials. m counts nodal lines crossing the x-axis (i.e. lobes along x), n along y; p counts dark rings and |l| the azimuthal order. Some texts write the LG indices in the order (l, p) or use TEMpl; check the convention before comparing labels.

The mode order N = m + n (HG) or N = 2p + |l| (LG) sets the Gouy phase. In a resonator of length L the transverse-mode frequencies are ν = (c/2L)[q + (N + 1)·arccos(±√(g₁g₂))/π], so all modes with equal N are frequency-degenerate and any combination of them is again a mode: e.g. LG0±1 = (HG10 ± i·HG01)/√2. A superposition of modes with different N changes shape along z because their relative Gouy phase (NB − NA)ψ(z) changes.

Propagation. The complex beam parameter 1/q = 1/R − iλ/(πw²) transforms as q' = (Aq + B)/(Cq + D), with free space [[1, d], [0, 1]] and thin lens [[1, 0], [−1/f, 1]]. Because the HG and LG modes are shape-invariant and share the same q, a mode of order N has second-moment radius W(z) = √M²·w(z) with M² = 2m + 1 (x-direction of HGmn) or 2p + |l| + 1 (LG). Hence W₀θ = M²λ/π: for the same waist the far-field angle is √M² times larger, and behind a lens the focused spot is √M² times larger than for TEM00. The focus lies where Re q = 0 after the lens; for d₁ ≫ zR it approaches the focal plane and w₀' ≈ λf/(πwlens). The side view normalises each z-column to its own maximum so the lobe structure stays visible through the focus.

Re E and the cross-sections are normalised to the maximum |E| in the frame; the cross-sections run horizontally and vertically through the pixel of highest intensity; the phase map is faded out where |E| is below a few per cent of the maximum, where the phase is meaningless. M² = 2m + 1 (x), 2n + 1 (y) for HG and 2p + |l| + 1 for LG. The OAM readout is ⟨ℓ⟩ = ∫E*(−i∂φE) dA / ∫|E|² dA, evaluated numerically on the displayed grid.
