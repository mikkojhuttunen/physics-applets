FYS.501 Laser Physics

1. Introductory Concepts

  • Laser Condition and Logarithmic Losses (threshold inversion, round-trip gain)
  • Temporal and Spatial Coherence: Michelson and Young

2. Semiclassical Theory of Light–Matter Interaction

  • Laser Gain Media: Absorption & Emission
  • Line Broadening and Spectral Hole Burning
  • Field Strength Console: Laser Intensity Regimes

3. Passive Optical Resonators

  • Gaussian Beam Cavity Explorer (ABCD resonator stability)
  • Transverse Modes: Hermite–Gauss and Laguerre–Gauss (field, phase, intensity, M² focusing)

4. Continuous Wave (CW) Behaviour of a Laser

  • Laser Rate Equations: 3- and 4-level systems (ruby, Nd:YAG)
  • Spatial Hole Burning in a Standing-Wave Cavity (linear vs ring)
  • Mode Competition: homogeneous vs inhomogeneous gain, etalon mode selection
  • Optimum Output Coupling: output power vs mirror transmission
