Gaussian beam propagation in optical cavities

Round-trip ABCD matrices build the propagator; the self-consistent complex beam parameter q is solved from it directly, the way you'd do it for a real resonator — for FYS.501 Laser Physics. Configurations follow the standard types on Wikipedia's optical cavity page.

Resonator mode

Method: the round trip M = R₁·P(L)·R₂·P(L) is built from mirror matrices R = [[1,0],[−2/R,1]] and propagation matrices P(L) = [[1,L],[0,1]]. Self-consistency q = (Aq+B)/(Cq+D) gives q(z) = (z − zwaist) + i·zR, with zR = √(1−m²)/|C| and m = (A+D)/2 — the standard resonator eigenmode construction (Kogelnik & Li).
