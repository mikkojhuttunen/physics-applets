Michelson interferometer

A photodetector collects all light from the source while the path difference δ = 2·(arm difference) is scanned. Bandwidth limits the contrast through τ = δ/c; a finite source size limits it through the spread of ray angles θ.

Young's double slit

Two slits separated by s sample the field at two points. Contrast at the centre of the screen (δ = 0) is set by spatial coherence alone; away from the centre the path difference δ = s·y/R grows and bandwidth washes the fringes out.

Formulas used

Temporal. With τ = δ/c and FWHM Δν: Lorentzian exp(−πΔν|τ|), Gaussian exp(−(πΔντ)²/(4 ln 2)), top-hat |sinc(πΔντ)|. A multimode laser with N equal modes spaced c/2Lcav and per-mode width Δν multiplies the Lorentzian by |sin(Nπτc/2Lcav) / (N sin(πτc/2Lcav))|, which revives the fringes at δ = 2Lcav, 4Lcav, ... The coherence time is quoted as τc = 1/Δν and the coherence length as Lc = cτc.

Spatial, Young. A uniform circular source of angular diameter α = w/D gives |γ12| = |2J₁(x)/x| with x = π s α/λ (van Cittert–Zernike). The first zero and the coherence width are at s ≈ 1.22 λD/w.

Spatial, Michelson. Rays at angle θ have path difference δ cos θ ≈ δ(1 − θ²/2). Averaging over a disc of half-angle θm = w/2D gives |sinc(πδθm²/2λ)|. This is for a detector that collects the whole source; it is the reason an extended source limits the resolution of a Fourier-transform spectrometer.

Combining. The two factors are multiplied. This is exact only for narrow lines, but is a good guide for the broad sources here. Curves beyond the sinc envelope are drawn as the envelope to avoid aliasing on the log axis.

Things to try.

  • Lamp preset, untick spatial: Michelson contrast is now set by bandwidth only, and the fringes vanish within a few µm. Then untick temporal instead and tighten the source size.
  • Single-mode laser: Michelson contrast stays near 1 up to hundreds of metres, and Young fringes fill the screen at any s.
  • Lamp in Young: only a few fringes around the centre are visible even with a point source (temporal). Now enlarge the slit separation with a wide source and the contrast collapses everywhere (spatial).
  • Mode comb: scan δ over 2Lcav and watch the fringes come back.
