Line broadening and spectral hole burning

FYS.501 Laser Physics, chapter 2. A single monochromatic laser saturates a gain line that is broadened homogeneously, inhomogeneously, or both. Drag the red marker in the upper plots, or use the sliders.

Inversion distribution N(ν₀)

How many atoms with resonance frequency ν₀ remain inverted.

Probe gain spectrum G(ν)

Gain a weak probe beam would see at frequency ν.

Gain at the laser frequency versus intensity

Slope of the curve reveals the broadening type: 1/(1+S) for homogeneous, 1/√(1+S) for inhomogeneous.

Parameters

All frequencies in GHz, measured from the line centre. Widths are FWHM.
