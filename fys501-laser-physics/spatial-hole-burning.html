Model and conventions

z is measured from the mirror at z = 0 and normalised to the cavity length L. A mode with number q has Iq(z) = S·Isat·sin²(qπz/L), so S is its peak intensity in units of Isat and its mean over a full period is S/2. Modes of different frequency add incoherently. Local saturation of the inversion is N(z) = N₀/[1 + I(z)/Isat]; the amber area is the inversion left unused, the blue area between N(z) and N₀ is what was extracted.

The extracted fraction is η = 1 − ⟨N⟩/N₀ averaged over the gain medium (the stimulated-emission power is proportional to η). The ring cavity is drawn with the same mean intensity over the medium as the standing wave, so the comparison isolates the effect of the intensity pattern. Gain seen by mode i is Gi = ⟨sin²(qiπz/L)·N⟩/⟨sin²(qiπz/L)·N₀⟩. When the second mode is off, G₂ is the gain a weak probe at q + Δq would see. Real cavities have q ~ 10⁵–10⁶; small q is used here so that the nodes can be resolved.
