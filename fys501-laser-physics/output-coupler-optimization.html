Model and notation

Space-independent rate-equation model of a four-level, homogeneously broadened laser (Ch. 4). Per-pass logarithmic losses γi = −ln(1 − Li), γ₁ = −ln ρ₁, γ₂ = −ln ρ₂, and total loss per pass γ = γi + (γ₁ + γ₂)/2. Above threshold the output power is Pout = (γ₂/2) AbIs(Rp/Rcp − 1) (Eq. 4.26).

Since Rcp ∝ γ, the pump ratio is Rp/Rcp = g₀ℓ/γ, where g₀ℓ = σN0ℓ is the single-pass gain the medium would have with no lasing (N0 = Rpτ). With γ₂ = 0 the threshold is lowest, so xm = P/Pthmin = g₀ℓ/(γ₁/2 + γi). Setting dPout/ds = 0 gives sopt = √xm − 1 (Eq. 4.36). At the optimum the clamped gain equals γ = √(g₀ℓ·γmin), the geometric mean of the small-signal gain and the unavoidable loss.

The dashed curve is the total power drawn from the inversion by stimulated emission, AbIs(g₀ℓ − γ). A fraction γ₂/(2γ) of it (the output coupling efficiency ηout) leaves through the output coupler; the rest is dissipated in the internal loss and the back mirror. The extracted power is largest for T → 0, but then none of it comes out.

The plot is drawn against T, but γ₂ = −ln(1 − T) is used in the formulas, as in the lecture notes. For large T the intracavity intensity is no longer uniform along the cavity, and the space-independent model becomes only qualitative.
